{# ── Special characters ── #}
Special Characters
  • っ / ッ Small tsu — doubles the following consonant (geminate).
    e.g. きって (kitte = stamp), ざっし (zasshi = magazine)
  • ー Long vowel mark (katakana only) — extends the vowel.
    e.g. コーヒー (kōhī = coffee), ケーキ (kēki = cake)
{# ── Particle pronunciation ── #}
Particle Pronunciation Exceptions

Three hiragana are pronounced differently when used as particles:

  • は written ha, read wa as the topic marker particle
  • へ written he, read e as the direction particle
  • を written wo, read o as the object marker particle
